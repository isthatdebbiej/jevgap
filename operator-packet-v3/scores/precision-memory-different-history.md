## Reset

Prepare the five A/B/C/D/E arrow-labelled cubes and board targets as specified in the precision-memory proposal. Confirm ACT start pose and matched layout.

## Scoring

**full_success** — Were all 11 transfers completed in order, with correct final targets and yaw, stable releases, no early A/B/C moves and no manual assistance?

**distractors** — Were all eight D/E transfers completed in the prescribed order?

**memory** — Were the remembered object/target/orientation choices and their order correct, including any correction?

**precision** — Did all final placements meet the frozen centre/yaw tolerances and remain stable for two seconds after release?

**unassisted** — Was the rollout completed without manual assistance or early task-cube movement?
